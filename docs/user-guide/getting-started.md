### Power and charging

To turn on your Game Bub, press <span class="btn btn-power">Power</span>. A glowing purple light indicates the device is booting up.

To turn Game Bub off, press the <span class="btn btn-power">Power</span> and select "Power Off" from the menu. To forcibly turn off an unresponsive device, hold <span class="btn btn-power">Power</span> for 7 seconds or until the device turns off.

Charge the device via the USB-C port on the bottom with a 5V power supply that can supply at least 1.5A. A solid orange light indicates the device is charging, and the light will turn off when the device is fully charged.

### Navigating menus

The first time you turn on your Game Bub, you'll go through a quick button tutorial and a regulatory information page.

Use the <span class="btn btn-dpad">D-Pad</span>, <span class="btn btn-confirm">Confirm</span>, and <span class="btn btn-cancel">Cancel</span> buttons to navigate menus.

Use <span class="btn btn-vol-up">Volume+</span> and <span class="btn btn-vol-down">Volume-</span> to adjust the audio volume. While holding <span class="btn btn-home">Home</span>, press <span class="btn btn-vol-up">Volume+</span> and <span class="btn btn-vol-down">Volume-</span> to adjust the display brightness.
